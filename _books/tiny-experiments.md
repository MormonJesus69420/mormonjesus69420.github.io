Currently in progress, I was interested by it's premise and recommendation from Ryder Carroll, author of the "Bullet Journal Method".
I am enjoying the book so far, I want to try implementing some of its ideas in my life, but I am waiting until I have finished the book to do so.
I am particularly interested in the idea of turning my life into a series of experiments, and I am curious to see how this approach can help me to embrace uncertainty, take risks, and accept failure as a natural part of the process.
I can see how using small, low-stakes experiments to test out new ideas and approaches can be a beneficial way to learn and grow, and I am excited to see how this book can help me to apply this mindset in my own life.
